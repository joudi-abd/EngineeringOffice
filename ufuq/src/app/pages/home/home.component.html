About the quarter

A park address, drawn in glass.

Victory Park Residences is a residential quarter of curved glass towers set above a landscaped court. Daylight, long views, and a measured pace of construction.

Architecture

Rounded corners, floor to ceiling glass.

The main tower turns toward the light. Horizontal bands mark each floor, and the glass carries the sky down to the trees.

Location

A quiet edge of the park.

The buildings sit back from the street, with the court and trees in front and the city only as a distant edge.

Amenities

Courtyard, lobby, and private views.

A landscaped court, a calm lobby, and apartments planned around the long glazed walls of the tower.
